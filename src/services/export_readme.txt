Your NexusAI data export

export.json  your account details, reminders, tasks, notifications, the log of actions the assistant took for you,
             your usage, your saved conversations (the chat messages) and the list of your projects and files.
files/       the documents you uploaded, in a folder per project.

Not included, on purpose: your password (we only keep a scrambled form that cannot be turned back into it), the
tokens that let the app use your Google account, and the search index built from your documents (it can be rebuilt
from the files).
